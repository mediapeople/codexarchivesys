# Threaded Theater

A crawling record,
mutating algorithms,
like you see in the movies.

And in the next 36 seconds,
context will be quashed.

They’ll wake up
with a basic

one plus one equals

A motion.
A case.
An argument.

A missing contractual detail,
use,
and exercising inquiry.

Flower with babe,
smoke and terpenes,
alongside a conflated morality
turned duty.

30 minutes to sunrise,
and I’ve been up since yesterday.

The dogs and I,
working from our backs
in the sun.

It doesn’t go down
for the wicked.

I can feel the sting in my face.
At least I remember my name.

Recovery,
at tension with being
a mobile creature.

Training wheels.
We are all informing the other.

Steady now.
Stay on target.

29 minutes to interactive.

A sustained continuity
in a project floating
through uncharted space.

Can you feel me now?
Hear me?
Ride the lightning.

I have not been up this long
since Montana, ’94.

Flathead Valley,
and that white wizard found me.

We used to enjoy White Russians
through the drive-up window.

Hershey’s white chocolate,
those crumbled cookie bits.

Caramelize.
A change under heat.

The chemistry,
under power and direction.

The magic,
mankind and their medicines.

Another concoction
to see tomorrow.

A shanty vessel,
flying apart,
replenished with the parts
of our own soul.

21 minutes.

Don’t miss standup.
You won’t forgive yourself.

The dogs have been snatched up.

The thirdborn,
vocalizing his tasks
to the air,

but I know
he knows
I’m listening.

His suboperations,
audible.

The flying apart
in real time.

His announcements,
the patching
he feels compelled to do.

Because forgetting
would be catastrophe.

The world,
this house of cards,
could come crashing down quick

if you misdirect me.

But not everything
is coming apart.

Some things
he cannot wait
to tell me.

A schedule,
an anticipation,
the necessary stakeholders
privy and situated.

Threaded theater.

The thirdborn
announces his intentions
to the air.

A house of cards,
a cast of stakeholders,

and tomorrow
already rehearsing
its entrance.

13 minutes to midnight.
