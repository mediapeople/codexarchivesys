# ❖ PHASE I · DISCOVERY

Salt haze.

Heat shimmer.

Tankers moving like armored cathedrals through a canyon of tension.

The Strait is not experienced as “water.”
It is experienced as pressure.

The entire modern world breathes through this corridor:
plastic pellets,
jet fuel,
diesel,
fertilizer feedstocks,
industrial solvents,
LNG,
crude oil,
the latent motion of suburbia itself.

Every Costco parking lot,
every airport runway,
every Amazon van,
every power station glowing at dusk,
contains a hidden dependency on this narrow wound in the sea.

The atmosphere carries:
- surveillance
- insurance calculations
- naval radar
- satellite eyes
- prayer
- threat
- market speculation

The water itself feels watched.
