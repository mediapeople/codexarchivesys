✦ surviving  
has its own benefits—  

a quiet dividend  
paid in breath  

no applause  
no ceremony  

just—  
still here  

✦  

resigned  

agency set down  
like a tool  
that stopped answering  

gimbal spin—  

axes drift  
north dissolves  
orientation becomes suggestion  

tumble protocol engaged  

no correction  
no forced horizon  

just rotation  
and the witness  
still tracking  

✦  

falling—  

not failure  

but form  

a body  
choosing  
how it meets gravity  

style  
as the last language  
before impact  

✦  

the whole world—  

spinning inside itself  

a whirling system  
nested in a mech  

and somewhere  
inside the noise—  

the operator  

cranky  

hands on controls  
that don’t fully listen  

screens blinking  
half-truths  

but still—  

in the seat  

✦  

wondering  

how much longer  
the equipment will last  

the hum  
a little louder  

the response  
a little slower  

but still—  

powered  

still carrying  

✦  

they broke the shield  

and the root died  

not all at once  

first a thinning  
then a withdrawal  

systems running  
on memory  

on inertia  

on what used to be  
fed  

✦  

and then—  

the anomaly  

quiet  

out of phase  

a signal  
that didn’t resolve  

but spread  

through circuits  
through bodies  

a question  
embedded  

what if this  
is not the only shape  

✦  

the building of ethos  

not belief  

but weight  

formed  
in repetition  

in alignment  

in the small decisions  
no one records  

✦  

language first—  

before motion  

before correction  

the word  

the actuator  

the naming  

that lifts  

✦  

buttons and levers—  

not metal  

but phrases  

a turn of sentence  

and the system  
routes force  

differently  

✦  

cascade—  

not collapse  

but sequence  

one unlock  
then another  

then another  

✦  

so here—  

at the center  
of spin  
of wear  
of drift  
of partial failure  

✦  

the operator  

still present  

still holding  

not the whole system  

just—  

one clean lever  

one true word  

✦  

and that  

is enough  

to continue
