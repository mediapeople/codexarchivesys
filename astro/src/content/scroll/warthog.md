✦ SPECIMEN ✦

Warthog  
Phacochoerus africanus

✦ FIELD READ ✦

Carries its own contradiction.  
Grotesque at distance. Precise up close.  

Kneels to feed, rises to bolt.  
Tail lifts like a signal tower—  
not elegance, but broadcast.

✦ STRUCTURE ✦

form: low-slung mass, tusk-forward geometry, armored face plates  
system: root → chew → scan → run  
environment: open scrub, hardpan, burrow thresholds  
interface: mud layer (thermal regulation + parasite defense)

✦ PRESSURE ✦

Exposure in open ground.  
Predation from speed, not stealth.  

Sun as adversary.  
Teeth as constant negotiation.

✦ METHOD ✦

Eats close to the earth.  
Reads vibration through hoof and jaw.  
Uses abandoned holes—  
inherits architecture rather than builds it.

Runs with signal raised.  
Returns when the field forgets.

✦ MYTH ✦

Not built to be admired.  
Built to persist through ridicule of form.  

A face shaped by resistance.  
A body edited by consequence.  

The field does not crown it—  
the field fails to remove it.
