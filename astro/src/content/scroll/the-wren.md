Classification:
Small bird.

Large consequence.

---

Average Mass:

A handful of feathers.

A coin purse of bone.

Enough matter
to convince the wind
to carry it.

---

Skeletal Structure:

Delicate.

Efficient.

The architecture of a thing
that has signed an agreement
with gravity
without ever intending
to obey it completely.

---

Heart:

Disproportionate.

A recurring condition
among creatures
required to remain alert.

The heart appears oversized
for the body.

Or perhaps

the body appears undersized
for the heart.

Further study required.

---

Song Apparatus:

Located within the throat.

Functions as both communication
and declaration.

The species demonstrates
a remarkable tendency
to announce itself

despite possessing
every evolutionary reason
to remain quiet.

---

Tail:

Frequently elevated.

Purpose uncertain.

May function as balance.

May function as confidence.

May function as both.

---

Locomotion:

Rarely walks.

Prefers hopping.

Suggesting a worldview
fundamentally optimistic
about the distance
between things.

---

Behavior:

Investigates everything.

Distrusts nothing for long.

Capable of treating
the entire world

as unfinished business.

---

Observed Contradiction:

The bird appears fragile.

The bird behaves otherwise.

---

Predators:

Numerous.

Historical outcome:

The wrens remain.

---

Nest Construction:

An exercise in improbable engineering.

Twigs.

Fibers.

Leaves.

Persistence.

A recurring pattern
throughout nature.

---

Field Notes:

The species demonstrates
an unusual relationship
with scale.

Observers consistently report
that the bird appears larger

while present

than measurements later confirm.

---

Working Theory:

Size is partially physical.

Partially narrative.

The wren exceeds its dimensions
through force of character.

---

Conclusion:

Not all creatures survive
through strength.

Some survive

through curiosity.

Some survive

through attention.

Some survive

through carrying a song

too large

for the body assigned to it.
