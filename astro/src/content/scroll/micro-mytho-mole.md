## ✦ SPECIMEN ✦

Eastern Mole  
*Scalopus aquaticus*

## ✦ FIELD READ ✦

Lives where sight fails.  
Builds velocity through resistance.  
Turns obstruction into habitat.

## ✦ STRUCTURE ✦

- form: spade-shaped forelimbs
- system: constant tunneling
- environment: dense soil, low light

## ✦ PRESSURE ✦

Total visual irrelevance.

## ✦ METHOD ✦

Does not navigate by seeing.  
Navigates by *touch saturation*.  
Moves forward by collapsing what is in front of it.

## ✦ MYTH ✦

The blind engineer.  
The one who trusts pressure over image.  
A life built entirely inside the unseen.

## ✦ TRANSFER ✦

When vision fails, increase contact.  
Progress is sometimes the act of making space by force.
