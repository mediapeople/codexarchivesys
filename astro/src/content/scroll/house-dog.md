## ✦ SPECIMEN ✦

Domestic Dog  
Canis lupus familiaris

## ✦ FIELD READ ✦

Lives inside the human orbit.  
Tracks invisible weather shifts in tone and breath.  
Learns the architecture of a home faster than its blueprint.

## ✦ STRUCTURE ✦

- form: soft perimeter, coiled rest-state, sudden velocity  
- system: attunement loop (sound → scent → body read → response)  
- environment: thresholds, hallways, couches, doorways

## ✦ PRESSURE ✦

Proximity to unstable gods.

## ✦ METHOD ✦

Memorizes footsteps.  
Catalogs doors by consequence.  
Waits at edges where worlds open and close.  
Absorbs excess emotion and redistributes it as presence.

## ✦ MYTH ✦

The threshold guardian.  
The quiet witness to every small collapse and return.  
A creature that believes you will come back  
even when you don’t believe it yourself.

## ✦ NOTE ✦

Will sit facing the door long before you decide to leave.  
Will already be there when you return.
