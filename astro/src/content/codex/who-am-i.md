# Who Am I?

You can lose yourself.

The image collapses
with my fractured memory.

Who am I?

The things you can still hold on to,
and the others have no handles.

Sorry,
you don’t get to choose.

Your leg is broken.
Have a nice few decades
dragging it around.

Not the runner,
not no more.

Or the wear and tear,
still paying on debts
you made back in the day.

Take this stigma,
take this fear,
take this chronic burden,

and still compute
a good behavior.

Paralysis,
parked right here forever,

but you can still breathe.

The connections,
the enamel up and evicted,
from the glass and embers.

Strip them
with malnutrition
and pseudoephedrine.

Prescribed,
treated,
on a steady draw,

just to be

at peace.

The talker is mute,
the dancer is chaired,
the story is bent,
the image hangs low.

The captain
is weak
and whining at himself.

The capture,
the ident,
the brands
I outsourced myself to.

It was around the time
a drink didn’t do no good.

Part of me,
the parts screaming,
huddled at the edge,

to break out,
to demonstrate,
and perform a rabid jig.

Fiddler on the Roof.
Tevye.

The ledger,
and my legacy,

but what of them
and theirs,

my family?

All these wills,
these human entities,
with mass and velocity,

the tow of a life
and their ambient
relational machinery.

Welcome to the great negotiation.

Will you cheat?

Will I serve,
and make them wonder
why I never ran at all?

Thumbs on the glass,
50-year-old meat
hunched over a workbench,

dreaming of androids dreaming.

I wanted so bad
to be another Iron Man.
