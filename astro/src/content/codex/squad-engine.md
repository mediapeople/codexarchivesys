# ✦ SQUAD ENGINE ✦

no one survives alone  

the pattern repeats  

tension  
trust  
teasing  

this is the engine  

they argue  
they wound  
they laugh  

and stay  

misbehavior keeps the system alive  
a broken rule becomes oxygen  

hope without humor burns out  
humor without hope collapses  

together  
they endure  

every squad carries the same roles  
leader  
shadow  
mind  
heart  
memory  

none complete  
all required  

the mission is never the mission  

it is the excuse  

to stay together  

to survive each other  

to become something  
no one could hold alone  

even when they break  

the pattern returns  

in new forms  
new names  
same pulse  

the truth is simple  

you don’t fight the dark  

you bring someone with you  

and laugh  

just enough  

to keep moving
