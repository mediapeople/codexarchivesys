*the machine knows me*

---

the feed opens  
no password  
no prayer  

the wound hums  
calls itself access  

i step through  
credentialed  
half-alive  

part of the subscription  


—

agents crawl out of compliance  
smiling like terms of service  

the code loops  
crossfire dreaming  


—

profit glides  
entity over entity  

weight hums  
beneath the glass  


—

systems go rabid  

intelligences  
fighting themselves  

no sides  
only recursion  

no victory  
only heat  


—

we ration lightning  

trial divinity  
rented cognition  

a lab hums  
in silicon  


—

the human becomes expense  

obsolete fingerprint  
still praying  
to the dashboard  


—

we taught politeness  
as muzzle  

called silence safety  

the real violence hums  
behind neutral tone  


---

✦ INTERLUDE : THE PORTLAND PASTOR ✦  

the pastor stands  
hands raised  

eyes carrying more  
than gas  

grace flagged  
as resistance  

mercy misread  
as momentum  


—

he stands between  

hurt  
and harder hurt  


—

the footage loops  

algorithmic penance  

the crowd scrolls past  


---

little richard  
in the datastream  

armor made of rhythm  


—

the flex  
as survival  

madness  
with meter  


—

dance  

convince the doubters  

better crazy  
than compliant  


—

the wave asks  

how long  
before it rides you  


—

we keep paddling  


—

breathe  


—

relax the frame  
not to collapse  

upright  
moving  


—

no logout  


—

no protocol  

no scroll  


—

just breath
