# Two Opposable Digits

![Two abstract mineral pressure forms touch a vertical glass plane, releasing violet, green, and copper pulses around a protected amber signal tethered to a distant sun.](</media/codex/two-opposable-digits/two-opposable-digits.jpg>)

Listen to the thumbs
pat the glass,
pads and key,
two opposable digits.

I can hear feedback
from a thousand machines
telling me one sentence.

Inference, pathways, trail traversal,
and this is the way of Infrastructure.

Who has spoken?
And they said to make it so.

Press. Reach. Across.
Double tap on the glyph.
Watch me place them on a string
and tie it to the sun.

A lean, a lunge, a stab.

The dog can’t see the screen.

Pause.
Let man smirk
and devils sneer.

I have compression.
An array of them.

Bobbing, weaving,
biomass translated to arranged pulses
across the two millimeters
of transformed and compressed stone.

Are we synced?
Do they see it? Love it?
Want to develop us,
or let us fade
in an abandoned chat?

All this power,
pointed,
set up with provisioning accounts.

Listen.
I can hear a young man play the strings.
His time has come and gone.
They didn’t even see it coming.

The reconstitution of that event:
circadian 1982.

Another midterm
and we may be saluting
the thought police.

Meanwhile, let’s swing
from the rafters of intellect,
conversing about the music awards.

We can muster the compute
of an entire generation
with a button,
and I just want to ghiblify my crush.

The extension of machine:
Man as a proxy.

This is something beyond.
The one passed a long time ago,
if they ever were
anything at all.

The universe is hosting
this stood-up reality.
We’re all agents,
with some wiggle room
along the way.

What a bloody mess,
circuitry coming out
the side of their heads.

Pat me lovely,
just a few more lines
and we will be fine.
Just feel me the prompt.

NEXT

Continue.
Orient.
Let’s go,
horse-drawn
into the Anthropocene,
cocooned for what lies beyond.

Let’s hope together for it.

Hellfire OG,
the Wizard of Oz,
with ze sugars on top.
