# Machined Agreements

I can feel the purr
of machined agreements.

Separate parts,
each holding its tolerance,
each refusing a little
of the force.

Power contained
and channeled into control.

Enough force
to put us down.

Instead,
we launch across the room
at excessive speeds

and arrive
without injury.

Something fragile
rides inside the violence.

Every bearing
keeps its word.
