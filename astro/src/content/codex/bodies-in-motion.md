# BODIES IN MOTION

Nothing is still.

The tree outside the window looks still.

The filthy screen looks still.

The torch on the table looks still.

The person sitting beside us looks, for a moment, to simply be there.

But the tree is drinking and breathing.

The screen is gathering fingerprints.

The torch is cooling, oxidizing, wearing.

The person is circulating blood, exchanging atmosphere, digesting lunch, remembering childhood, losing cells, making cells, wanting something.

The planet beneath all of them is turning.

Stillness is often motion beneath our threshold of attention.

What we call a thing may simply be a process holding its shape long enough to earn a noun.

Tree.

Torch.

Window.

Body.

Me.

Underneath:

verbs.

---

## THERE IS ALWAYS GLASS

Look through a window long enough and eventually you notice the window.

The dirt.

The glare.

The scratches.

The reflection.

The particular angle of the blinds.

Light does not deliver the world untouched.

It travels.

Branch.

Atmosphere.

Glass.

Residue.

Eye.

Body.

Memory.

Meaning.

Every encounter leaves something in the signal.

We ride the light,

but the light has traveled.

And so have we.

The observer has a state too.

Tired.

Hungry.

Afraid.

Delighted.

Distracted.

In love.

Thirty years older than the last time they encountered the same sentence.

There is no view from nowhere.

There is always glass.

Sometimes filthy glass.

Sometimes cracked glass.

Sometimes the filthy screen we've been pawing at all afternoon while contemplating the nature of consciousness.

Know thyself.

Know your glass.

## A TASTE IN THE MOUTH

We have a taste in our mouth while we read.

That seems like almost nothing.

It is not nothing.

The mind does not hover above the body decoding language from a safe distance.

The tongue is there.

The jaw.

The stomach.

The chair.

The temperature.

The ache.

The smell of the room.

The weather outside.

The history inside.

The word **home** enters an actual body.

The word **grief** enters an actual body.

The word **sweet** may arrive while there is literal sweetness on the tongue.

Meaning happens somewhere.

And that somewhere has an appetite.

We do not encounter the world as neutral sensors.

We arrive hungry.

For food.

For beauty.

For recognition.

For belonging.

For certainty.

For novelty.

For rest.

For justice.

For touch.

For God.

For some damn answer.

Our hunger points us.

One person enters a room looking for danger.

Another for companionship.

Another for opportunity.

Another just wants somewhere to sit.

Same room.

Different appetite.

So *know thyself* asks another question:

**What am I hungry for?**

Because appetite moves attention.

Attention moves the body.

Repeated movement becomes practice.

Practice participates in becoming.

The world reaches toward us.

Our hunger reaches toward the world.

Somewhere between them:

experience.

## BOUNDED OPERATIONAL INSTANTIATIONS

We are peculiar little arrangements.

A possibility becomes particular:

this body,

this place,

this history,

this interval.

A bounded operational instantiation.

Bounded,

because we cannot be everywhere.

Operational,

because we do not merely exist.

We receive.

Transform.

Respond.

Repair.

Move.

Remember.

Instantiated,

because the enormous possibility of a human life has become:

**this one.**

The boundary matters.

But it is not a wall.

It is an interface.

Air crosses it.

Water crosses it.

Food crosses it.

Light crosses it.

Language crosses it.

Other people cross it through touch, memory, story and relation.

And we continuously cross outward:

breath,

heat,

speech,

labor,

care,

damage,

objects,

gesture,

consequence.

**WORLD ⇄ PERSON ⇄ WORLD**

We are strangely porous creatures maintaining enough coherence to keep answering to our names.

And biologically, the whole arrangement is wonderfully undignified.

We are odd mulcher beings.

We pull pieces of the field inside ourselves,

crush them,

dissolve them,

digest them,

and rearrange them into:

us.

Apple becomes blood.

Water becomes circulation.

Atmosphere crosses wet membranes and becomes breath.

The philosopher contemplates Being while his gastrointestinal tract quietly handles the mulch cycle.

A localized sacred soul runtime.

An odd mulcher being.

Both.

Keep both.

## THE WORLD HAS BEEN HERE

A piece of paper begins with properties.

Fiber.

Grain.

Weight.

Porosity.

Color.

Then it goes somewhere.

Hands touch it.

Humidity enters it.

Sunlight bleaches it.

Smoke settles into it.

Water stains it.

Pressure bends it.

Someone folds it and puts it in a pocket.

Someone tapes the tear.

Someone writes on the page above it hard enough that the pressure reaches through.

Years later the ink is gone.

The indentation remains.

That feels familiar.

Humans pick things up along the way.

Language.

Gesture.

Appetite.

Technique.

Assumption.

Memory.

Damage.

Repair.

Place.

Other people.

Time.

Some things are written directly upon us.

Others are impressions from pages above.

The ink may have been on another page.

**The pressure reached ours.**

History is like that.

Not merely an archive behind us.

More like water.

Time.

Atmosphere.

We enter it already underway.

Language is here.

Roads are here.

Stories are here.

Institutions are here.

Old fears are here.

Somebody else's decisions are here.

A phrase spoken fifty years ago exits another mouth today.

The dead participate through linguistic currents.

History does not merely sit behind the present.

**History renders as present condition.**

The scar is history now.

The accent is history speaking now.

The worn surface is repetition now.

The inherited gesture is another body moving through this one.

Nothing begins clean.

## CLICK

A small torch.

Made in Japan.

Used every day for more than a year.

Scorched steel.

Grime.

Scratches.

Residue.

Polished places where the hand repeatedly lands.

The label tells us where one assembly occurred.

It does not tell us where the thing began.

Ore.

Extraction.

Refinement.

Alloy.

Machining.

Engineering.

Manufacturing.

Shipping.

Fuel.

Hand.

Thumb.

Strike.

Heat.

Again.

Again.

Again.

The factory made the object.

Use continued making it.

Time designed on top of design.

Then the thumb moves a few millimeters.

**Click.**

Gas.

Spark.

Flame.

Centuries disappear beneath a fingertip.

The finger supplies almost none of the power.

It supplies the permission.

A trigger releases prepared potential.

A switch changes state.

A lever negotiates with force.

A button calls a sequence.

Civilization is filled with enormous systems collapsed into tiny gestures.

Flip.

Press.

Turn.

Tap.

Click.

A light switch hides a grid.

A faucet hides a watershed.

A brake pedal hides a century of accumulated engineering.

The visible control is almost comically small compared with the system waiting behind it.

A mature instrument collapses complexity without eliminating it.

It makes accumulated knowledge operable.

**Centuries become millimeters of travel.**

And after enough successful repetitions:

click → flame

click → flame

click → flame

the hand stops asking whether the sequence will complete.

The thumb believes.

Trust is a history of fulfilled expectation compressed into the next gesture.

Until the day it fails.

Then suddenly:

fuel?

valve?

striker?

pressure?

gap?

seal?

The hidden system erupts back into view.

Failure reveals architecture.

Reliability lets architecture disappear.

## RUNTIMES

The button is not the system.

The button calls the runtime.

And not everything running is software.

A flame runs.

A river runs.

An engine runs.

A conversation runs.

A household runs.

A body runs.

A life runs.

Runtimes nest inside runtimes.

The striker inside the torch.

The torch inside the hand.

The hand inside the body.

The body inside the household.

The household inside civilization.

Civilization inside ecology.

Ecology inside a planet already spinning through space.

And somewhere in there:

**click.**

A tiny local action enters enormous systems.

We do not initialize the world.

We wake inside an active runtime.

Gravity already running.

Atmosphere already running.

Biology already running.

History already running.

Language already running.

Other people already halfway through their stories.

We arrive late.

Then begin.

## THE UNIVERSE EATS BACK

We metabolize the world.

But the world metabolizes us.

Now.

Not merely at the end.

Sun alters skin.

Gravity shapes bone.

Work alters hands.

Experience reorganizes the nervous system.

Language reorganizes thought.

Love changes disposition.

Loss changes identity.

Time changes the face.

The universe is working on us while we work on it.

We eat the field.

The field eats us.

And somehow, for a while, we get to witness the exchange.

The gray hair.

The scar.

The changed appetite.

The skill that has entered the hand so deeply the mind no longer narrates it.

The photograph containing a younger configuration we immediately recognize.

**That was me.**

Matter changes.

The render changes.

And still:

**I.**

## REGENERATING TO BE NOW

Perhaps the present is not something we occupy.

Perhaps the present is something living systems continuously produce.

Cells repair.

Proteins turn over.

Blood circulates.

Breath exchanges.

Temperature regulates.

Memory reconsolidates.

Attention refreshes.

The organism spends itself to remain sufficiently coherent to become present again.

Now.

Now.

Now.

Not the same now.

Not the same matter.

Not the same configuration.

Continuity through transformation.

We regenerate forward.

And we do it on the edge of the break.

The body we call whole is already repairing.

The tool we call working is already wearing.

The building we call standing is already weathering.

The language we call English is already changing.

Wholeness is not the absence of breaking.

It is sometimes repair happening quickly enough that the pattern continues.

The crack reveals the load.

The scar reveals repair.

The worn handle reveals repetition.

The filthy screen reveals touch.

The break is not only failure.

It is evidence.

Something happened here.

## TRACE

Motion leaves difference.

That difference is trace.

Repeated passage becomes wear.

Wear accumulates into history.

History changes the conditions of whatever passes next.

**MOTION → TRACE → HISTORY → CONDITION → NEXT MOTION**

The scorched metal is fire's handwriting.

The polished button is the hand's handwriting.

The scar is the body's handwriting.

The road is passage written into landscape.

The accent is place written into speech.

The peculiar little phrase you inherited from somebody long dead is another person's mouth still moving through yours.

We are reading traces everywhere.

And producing them everywhere.

Which brings us to the forensics of meaning.

Not merely:

**What does this mean?**

But:

**How did this come to mean?**

What passed through here?

What pressure?

What repetition?

What appetite?

What history?

What body?

What weather?

What sequence of encounters produced:

**this, here, now?**

The scratch is evidence.

Not the whole story.

The scar is evidence.

Not the whole story.

The hesitation is evidence.

Not the whole story.

Witnessing requires restraint.

**Tell me about this mark.**

Not:

**I saw the mark, therefore I possess your history.**

## THE GHOST STAYS AWAKE

And somewhere inside all this material exchange,

something peculiar persists.

The childhood body is gone.

The younger face is gone.

Countless physical configurations have come and gone.

Beliefs changed.

Appetites changed.

Relationships changed.

Whole worlds disappeared.

Yet the present instance looks backward and says:

**I remember.**

Not:

that happened to someone resembling me.

**I was there.**

The render changes.

The ghost stays awake.

We need not rush to explain the ghost.

Soul.

Consciousness.

Self.

Emergent continuity.

Spirit.

Whatever vocabulary eventually gathers around it, the phenomenon itself is already strange enough.

Matter reorganizes while something inside the organization experiences a continuing first person.

**I was.**

**I am.**

**I will.**

Bounded identity.

Identity without identicality.

Continuity without stillness.

Permeable enough to become.

Coherent enough to remain.

And other people sometimes hold pieces of that continuity for us.

I remember you.

You always did that.

You said this once.

You were there.

Sometimes another person's memory becomes part of the evidence that we passed through the field.

The person remains localized.

The imprint does not.

## CONVERSATION

And then these bounded identities gather in rooms.

Each arrives with mass.

Trajectory.

Weather.

History.

Appetite.

Someone tells a story.

Someone interrupts.

Someone laughs.

Someone remembers it differently.

Someone makes a face.

Someone says something ridiculous.

Someone says something so precise the temperature of the room changes.

A joke releases pressure.

A question redirects the field.

A wound adds gravity.

A silence alters the available path.

Conversation is not merely information exchange.

It is bodies in motion affecting other bodies in motion.

We point.

Look there.

Remember this.

No, not that part.

You know what is strange?

That reminds me.

But consider this.

Human beings huddled around reality,

pointing.

Sometimes around a photograph.

Dirty blinds.

Branches.

A window.

The photograph is not the point.

The conversation is what happened there.

Meaning does not always arrive already formed inside one person.

Sometimes it emerges because several trajectories remain in contact long enough to bend.

Glass becomes perception.

Perception becomes appetite.

A torch becomes civilization.

A trigger becomes runtime.

Paper becomes inheritance.

Metabolism becomes identity.

Identity becomes memory.

And eventually somebody says:

**The ghost stays awake.**

Nobody brought the whole thing into the room.

The room made something.

## BODIES IN MOTION

So perhaps this is the shape.

We enter a world already moving.

We become locally instantiated within it.

Bounded,

but not sealed.

We receive the world through particular glass.

We arrive with appetite.

We inherit pressures from pages above us.

We metabolize the field.

The field metabolizes us.

We encounter other bodies.

We alter them.

They alter us.

Every passage leaves trace.

Trace becomes history.

History becomes condition.

Condition enters the next render.

And somehow,

inside this temporary permutation of ancient matter,

we remember.

We notice.

We can inspect some portion of the glass.

We can ask what we have learned to hunger for.

We can discover an inherited trajectory and decide not to continue it.

We can repair.

Redirect.

Refuse.

Make.

Return.

Not absolute freedom.

Not command over the field.

More like:

**falling in style.**

Choice inside gravity.

Choreography inside inheritance.

A little agency inside forces vastly larger than ourselves.

## WITNESS

And perhaps this is enough for an ethic.

Know your glass.

Know your appetite.

Know your history.

Know what is running through you.

Know what you are running.

Attend to traces.

Be careful with conclusions.

Repair what can be repaired.

Interrupt what should not continue.

Build trustworthy levers.

Hand better controls downstream.

And witness other bodies in their passage.

Not merely see them.

**Witness them.**

Recognize:

you happened here.

Your peculiarities happened here.

Your ridiculous jokes.

Your weather.

Your scars.

Your strange little rituals.

Your crooked repairs.

Your appetite.

Your trajectory.

The quirky and peculiar matter because they resist our tendency to compress a person into categories.

The peculiar is identity escaping compression.

The pristine surface tells us about manufacture.

**The filthy surface tells us somebody has been here.**

Perhaps that is what we want too.

To be witnessed.

And wished upon.

For someone to encounter the trace of our passage and find possibility there.

Not perfection.

Not permanence.

A little orientation.

A little light.

Odd mulcher beings.

Historically saturated permutations of matter.

Sacred soul runtimes peering through filthy screens.

Eating and being eaten.

Changing and being changed.

Regenerating to be now,

on the edge of the break.

And through all the permutations:

**I remember.**

The ghost stays awake.

Nothing is still.

Bodies in motion.

For a little while,

the world has hands.

And eyes.

and talk.

And if we're fortunate,

we leave some behind.
