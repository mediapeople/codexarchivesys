# 420 Systems: From Curiosity to System

What began as a curiosity became a system.

420 Systems started with a simple question:

> Could one optimal surface create enough discipline for a product—without sanding away its personality?

The answer became a spatial system built around a fixed 420 cell.

That cell can divide into 210 and 105 derivatives, or combine into 840, 1260, and 1680 compositions. It does not stretch arbitrarily. It cascades, collapses, and recomposes according to the available space.

The package now includes:

- a responsive spatial model;
- dark, light, and high-contrast themes;
- production-ready components and states;
- multiple expression modes;
- applied examples and product demonstrations;
- a documented Figma source of truth.

The rule is consistent. The outcomes do not have to be.

The system can hold the peculiar.
