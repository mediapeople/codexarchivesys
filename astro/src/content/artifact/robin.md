## ✦ SPECIMEN ✦

American Robin  
Turdus migratorius

## ✦ FIELD READ ✦

Appears in the damp grass after rain.
Reads the surface for what the surface almost hides.
Turns the whole head to refine one invisible point.

## ✦ STRUCTURE ✦

* form: ember chest, lateral eye, short-hop ground body
* system: eye → tilt → foot → feel → strike
* environment: wet lawns, softened soil, edge zones, late April mornings

## ✦ PRESSURE ✦

The ground is speaking before the day hardens.

## ✦ METHOD ✦

Samples the lawn in short increments.
Presses its feet into softened earth.
Tilts until ambiguity narrows.
Waits without wasting stillness.
Strikes only when the hidden thing becomes exact.

## ✦ MYTH ✦

The field reader.
The damp-grass oracle.
A creature built to hear what rises beneath the visible world.

It does not wander.

It listens until the earth makes a small mistake.

## ✦ NOTE ✦

Late April, north Georgia.

Rain loosens.
Soil reveals.
Worm rises.
Robin reads.
