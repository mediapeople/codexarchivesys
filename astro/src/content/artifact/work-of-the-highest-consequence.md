# ✦ WORK OF THE HIGHEST CONSEQUENCE ✦

Not loud.

Not rushed.

Not begging to be seen.

This work does not ask for attention.
It assumes continuation.

—

Consequence is not importance.
It is aftershock.

It is the alteration of the field
after the act is complete.

A quiet reorganization.

A shift in what can be thought,
felt,
or made next.

—

This is not content.

Not output.

Not performance.

This is work that changes the conditions
under which all future work is received.

—

Work of the highest consequence
does not decorate the world.

It rearranges it.
