Mythplate is a fixed-layout doctrine plate about position, pressure, and authorship.

Its clearest line is the tension between two operating positions:

- the ground where everything shouts
- the sky where only one thing remains

The Counterfield is not chaos. It is proximity.

Too close, and every signal claims equal importance.
Urgency floods in.
Reaction takes the wheel.

The plate turns on a single law:

> rise, see, commit.

The eagle does not arrive as decoration.
It arrives as discipline.

- height before choice
- distance before reaction
- one clean selection instead of total intake

What Mythplate keeps insisting on is simple:

speed is not power

clarity is
