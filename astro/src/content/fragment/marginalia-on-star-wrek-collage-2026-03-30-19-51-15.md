✦ PRESSURE VESSEL / FRACTURED COMMAND ✦

they never left—

not really.

the ship holds
because it has to,

because everything else
has already broken.

voices split into angles,
commands issued mid-collapse,
hands steady
in a field that won’t stabilize.

this is not motion—

this is containment.

a story forced
to occupy itself
all at once.

edges hold.

glue holds.

paper remembers
what the frame
can’t organize.

and still—

the beams fire.

still—

the center answers.

not because it’s whole,

but because it refuses
to stop
being the place
everything passes through.

✦
