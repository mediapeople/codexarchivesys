This piece pulls from *The Death of Superman*, but it’s not trying to retell the moment.

Doomsday isn’t treated as a character here. He shows up as force.

Bone. Impact. Forward pressure that doesn’t negotiate.

Everything in the frame is caught mid-failure. The body is breaking apart, and the story is already trying to explain it at the same time.

Fragments of dialogue remain:
“HA HAAA”
“BONY PROTRUSIONS… SO SHARP… HE CUT ME!”
“BUT MOST WILL REMEMBER THIS SAD DAY—”

They don’t stabilize the moment. They lag behind it.

That’s the part that stuck with me.

The strike lands, and the memory of the strike begins forming immediately, even before anything resolves.

Built from original comic fragments and mounted at comic-book scale on board.

This one came together as a compression field more than a composition. No smoothing, no hiding seams. The cuts are the structure.

Part of the Torn Frames series.
