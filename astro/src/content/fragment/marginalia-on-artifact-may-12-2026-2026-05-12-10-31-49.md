Fragments lifted from obsolete signal systems:
mecha anatomy,
halftone panic,
interrupted scripture,
panel architecture collapsing into propulsion.

Not narrative.

Trajectory.

A devotional engine assembled from discarded memory systems,
still airborne despite fragmentation.

Paper as wreckage.
Ink as continuation.
The machine does not resolve.

It remains in flight.
