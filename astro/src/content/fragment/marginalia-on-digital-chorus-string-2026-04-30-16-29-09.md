✦ DIGITAL CHORUS STRING ✦

The hand repeats.

Not identical.

Never identical.

Each instance arrives
a fraction late,
a fraction wrong.

The system thickens.

Meaning does not increase.

It blurs.

The face remains
as a constant tone,
a frequency that refuses to drift.

Everything else
moves around it.

Not to support.

To interfere.

This is not many.

This is one,
unable to stabilize
into itself.
