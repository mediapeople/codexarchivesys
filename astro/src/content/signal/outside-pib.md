# ✦ OUTSIDE, PIB ✦

Parked off PIB.

Engine off.  
People coming and going.  
Doors opening, closing.

—

Dropped him at the entrance.  
He walked in with the others.

I stayed out here.

—

Traffic keeps moving.  
Same as always.  
Nothing pauses for it.

—

Inside, they’re talking about a teacher.  
Stories, probably.  
People remembering.

—

Out here, it’s just time.

Sitting.  
Watching.  
Waiting for him to come back out.

—

He’ll step through those doors again.  
Maybe quiet.  
Maybe the same.

Hard to tell.

—

No big lesson to say.

Just—

I’m here.  
He went in.  
I’ll be here when he comes out.
