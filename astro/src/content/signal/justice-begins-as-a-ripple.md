Justice rarely arrives as a monument.

It starts as a disturbance: a drop, a ring, a refusal to let the surface remain undisturbed. What looks small in the moment can still reorganize the whole field.

A clear word. A kept record. A witness who does not look away. These are not dramatic gestures at first, but they travel. They spread pressure into pattern.

Justice moves like this before it takes form in law or structure. First the signal breaks the silence. Then the signal repeats. Then others learn how to hear it.

Small enough to begin anywhere.
Strong enough to keep widening.
