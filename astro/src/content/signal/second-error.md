The first mistake
is noise—

a break in the line,
a slip,
a flare.

It announces itself.

The second mistake
is silence—

no turn,
no correction,
no receipt of signal.

Just forward
as if nothing spoke.

That’s where it sets—

not in the impact,
but in the carry.

Error is human.

Uncorrected error
is architecture.
